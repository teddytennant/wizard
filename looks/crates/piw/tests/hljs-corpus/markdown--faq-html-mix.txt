## FAQ

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Yes. Everything is stored in `~/.acme/` and synced when you're back online.

**Q: What about *Unicode* filenames?**
A: Fully supported: `résumé.pdf`, `日本語.txt`, `🚀.md`.
