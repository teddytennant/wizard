Subject: [PATCH 2/3] net: handle EAGAIN in the write loop

A short write on a non-blocking socket returned -EAGAIN and the caller
treated it as fatal, which closed healthy connections under load.
Retry after the poll wakes us instead.

Reported-by: 山田 太郎 <taro@example.jp>
Signed-off-by: Renée Dupont <renee@example.org>
